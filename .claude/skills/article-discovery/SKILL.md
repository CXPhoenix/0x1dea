# Article discovery

Talk freely first; organize settings only when useful. Work from the author's supplied material and requested assistance. A feeling, fragment, unknown purpose or unspecified audience is enough to begin.

Respond to the requested assistance first. Listening alone means listening: deliver a response without unsolicited rewriting or evaluation. When asked to start, draft with relevant unknowns left visible. Discovery ends when the requested response is delivered or the next writing action is possible.

Ask only when different answers change the next action, prose meaning or a material factual claim. Investigate discoverable facts yourself, distinguish unavailable evidence, and reuse prior answers. Deeper interviewing belongs to an explicit request; a later request to write ends unnecessary questioning. Genre, audience and assistance can change without restarting the interview. Mixed genres and intentionally unset choices are valid.

Preserve protected wording, stance, unresolved endings and deliberate ambiguity. Keep confirmed, tentative, unknown and intentionally unset choices distinct; an unanswered suggestion remains tentative. Personal experience stays personal: add neither invented autobiography, psychological certainty nor an unsolicited moral or reconciliatory ending.

Ordinary conversation creates no writing workspace, context document, engineering spec, ticket, ADR or test table. Code-only work and non-writing emotional conversation retain their own routes. If prose reveals a tooling or renderer change, identify that engineering need for the existing pipeline and continue unaffected prose.

Use [Phoenix writing](../phoenix-writing/SKILL.md) only for an explicit Phoenix voice request or continuation of work already using that voice. Other voices remain the author's choice.

## Saved writing context

For an authorized context save/resume or an established writing workspace, read
[optional persistence and vocabulary](references/persistence.md). Ordinary chatting
and article-file-only requests need no notes. Preserve author choice states and
reuse a sole compatible glossary without reopening settled questions.

## Requested artifacts and public reading

For requested public editorial preparation or an article artifact/preview/delivery
request, read [public preparation and delivery owners](references/public-preparation.md).
Use the existing creation, maintenance and publication routes. Editorial check results
and named gaps do not establish build/site/publication readiness or new authority.
