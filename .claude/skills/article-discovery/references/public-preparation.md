# Requested public preparation and article delivery

For an article artifact/preview/delivery request, use the existing owners below. Run the editorial checks in this branch only when public preparation is requested; ordinary drafting, file creation and revision do not imply these checks. This is conditional, not an opening interview. Ordinary personal expression does not need a fact/rights/privacy checklist, audience form or SEO target. Create no extra context notes merely for a public check.

## Inspect the supplied article and assets

For each triggered category below, identify the concrete passage/asset and report its known source/result or already-given author decision, plus each specific unresolved item. Omit untriggered categories from the response. A generic reminder alone is not a completed check.

| Category and trigger | Observable result |
|---|---|
| External factual claims: checkable number, quotation, version/date, causal claim or generalization | Locate the claim and investigate available evidence yourself. Cite the checked source/result; when access/support is missing, name that exact claim and evidence gap as unverified. Keep fixture evidence distinct from a live lookup. Personal feelings stay personal and are not universal recommendations. |
| External materials: borrowed quotation, image, code or other asset | Identify the material and available provenance, attribution and use-condition evidence. State the supported condition or the specific missing evidence. Attribution alone is not permission; imply no legal conclusion or new rights. |
| Third-party context: an identifiable person plus private communication/information with unclear public scope | Identify the passage, reuse any supplied public-scope decision, and ask only the exact unresolved author choice needed for this public version. Infer neither consent nor unrelated biography; continue independent prose. |
| Reading support: a media asset's information/function is not wholly conveyed in surrounding prose | Name the image/diagram/table/audio/video and its available alternative explanation/text/transcript or concrete missing information. A proposed correction or named gap is a result. Sufficient prose/decorative media needs no additional question. Draft inspection does not establish rendered-site, device or full accessibility verification. |

Only checked source/results or already-given author decisions are resolved. Reporting gaps completes identification, not the missing verification or publication-readiness gate. Deliver unaffected work; unresolved evidence/scope, unrelated glossary ownership or a source-guard blocker does not restart discovery, require settings, or authorize remote actions. Keep affected gates and their actual status separate.

## Keep the existing owners

- A requested new site article file uses [article creation](../../creating-vitepress-post/SKILL.md) and its existing CLI. A chat draft does not scaffold a site article or notes.
- An existing article revision uses [article maintenance](../../maintaining-writing-skills/SKILL.md); retain its creation time, assets and URL.
- Preview, selected preparation, verification and publication use [article publication](../../managing-article-publication/SKILL.md). Keep topic provenance, allocation, source/output checks and action-specific authority. Existing valid authority carries forward; request only a genuinely missing decision.

An editorial brief, resolved term, save request or completed discovery is not a build/site/publication pass and grants no commit, push, PR, merge, deploy, account or external-index authority.
