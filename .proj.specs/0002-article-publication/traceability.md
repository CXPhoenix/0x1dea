# Traceability

| Criterion | Type × field × boundary | Observable expected behavior |
|---|---|---|
| AP-01 | selected/article head/preview vs prepare | A+B preview, A-only candidate; source unchanged |
| AP-02 | article/ancestry/staging and other article | reject contamination including reverted content |
| AP-03 | dependency/path/ownership | strict schema, exact allowlist and safe regular files |
| AP-04 | receipt/pins and bytes/drift | invalidate on any changed pin or output |
| AP-05 | candidate/tree/conflict | stop with source untouched |
| AP-06 | input/instructions/authority | data only, no external or unauthorized actions |
| AP-07 | build/routes and content/unselected article | pinned-main exclusion algorithm, sandboxed build; missing signatures/proof blocks |
| AP-08 | skill/source/runtime | ADR-0005 ownership extension, native discovery, deterministic fourth English tree; Chinese exceptions unchanged |
| AP-09 | release/action/phase boundary | future exact authorization, current-main reviewed revert and new pinned re-publication candidate |
