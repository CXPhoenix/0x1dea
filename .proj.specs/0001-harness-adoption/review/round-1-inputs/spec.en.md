# Harness adoption specification

> G1/G4 approved by the user at 2026-10-04T07:24:41Z; independent review pending. Audit translation follows review.

## Problem Statement

0x1DEA needs the user's local harness workflow while preserving its working product and validated writing behavior. Historical Spectra evidence must remain truthful and accessible.

## Solution

Adopt the fixed template assets in an isolated checkout, preserve immutable product/history inputs, and expose one local epic/ticket tracker with reproducible physical runtime packages and verifiable evidence.

## User Stories

1. As the project owner, I want a single local tracker so task dependencies, approvals and evidence have one authority.
2. As a contributor, I want project skills discovered in a fresh runtime so writing and management do not depend on a global Phoenix installation.
3. As an auditor, I want immutable historical provenance and bilingual traceability so I can distinguish inheritance, supersession and unverified claims.
4. As a maintainer, I want complete regression and rollback evidence so management adoption preserves the existing product.

## Implementation Decisions

Use an independent clone pinned to the recorded staging baseline. Vendor the exact template snapshot with original licensing and unchanged framework prompts. Preserve canonical product writing skills; project them into two physical runtime trees with explicit metadata and relative-link transformations. Use a local tracker with globally allocated permanent ticket IDs, derived blockers and truthful landing state. Preserve all original history; retire only active legacy invocation surfaces into non-discoverable text archives. Record the narrowly scoped writing-language exception and existing-product adoption decisions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **HA-01:** Adoption SHALL run in an isolated checkout based on the recorded baseline; the original staging checkout and pinned template SHALL remain unchanged.
- **HA-02:** All six imported capabilities and four Spectra archives SHALL be indexed with their original Git baseline, paths and SHA-256 values. Historical artifacts SHALL NOT claim harness reviews, approvals, TDD or landing that did not occur.
- **HA-03:** New executable work SHALL use only the configured local `.proj.specs/` and `.proj.tickets/` tracker. Legacy Spectra invocation surfaces SHALL be inactive and retained as non-discoverable historical evidence.
- **HA-04:** The three product writing skills SHALL have one canonical editing source and deterministic regular-file runtime packages. Every repository reference SHALL resolve from its generated location. No symlink SHALL occur at any depth in either supported runtime's project skill tree.
- **HA-05:** Runtime packages SHALL retain invocation boundaries, required assets, licensing and vendor-specific metadata. Fresh native Codex discovery SHALL find the product and mandatory harness skills without a global product skill install.
- **HA-06:** Shared management instructions SHALL retain confirmed user gates and authorization boundaries. The existing product SHALL NOT claim a new-project walking-skeleton exemption. The writing language exception SHALL be explicit and scoped to named skills.
- **HA-07:** English specifications SHALL be authoritative; their Taiwan Traditional Chinese audit translations SHALL carry `synced_from` and a matching Git blob `synced_from_sha` before user audit. Every traceability row SHALL link an AC, ticket and real evidence or a declared unverified status.
- **HA-08:** Ticket IDs SHALL be unique, global, sequential and permanent. Stored status SHALL be one of todo/processing/review/done/pending; blocked SHALL be derived from dependencies. Missing dependencies, cycles, self-dependencies or incomplete pending metadata SHALL fail validation.
- **HA-09:** Baseline and candidate product sources SHALL remain byte-identical within the declared product allowlist. Unit, build, CLI, writing regressions and localhost UI SHALL be compared under recorded equivalent runtime/dependency conditions. Unexecuted checks SHALL NOT be reported as passed.
- **HA-10:** Adoption SHALL include a disposable-checkout recovery drill proving baseline content and skill entry restoration without modifying the original checkout or template. No commit, push, external write, deploy or permission change SHALL occur without separate authorization.
